Why Book Local

We're not a Vancouver operator running trips up on weekends - Squamish is home base, year-round. That means a captain who reads the day's conditions and wildlife activity instead of following a fixed script, and a company that answers the phone when plans change.

Always Private

Every tour we run is a private charter - it's your group and the captain, not a shared boat with strangers. That makes it easy to build the trip around what you actually want: photography stops, a slower pace with kids, or pushing further out for a full-day itinerary.

Getting Here

Tours depart from Squamish, an hour north of Vancouver on the Sea to Sky Highway. If you're coming from the city, ask about our sunrise and sunset tours departing directly from Porteau Cove instead.

What to Bring

Dress in layers - it's cooler on the water than on shore, even in summer. Sunglasses, sunscreen, and a camera are worth packing. Life jackets and all required safety equipment are provided.

Want to drive yourself instead? Check out our Squamish boat rentals - same RIB, no captain needed (valid boating licence required). Or grab a canoe or kayak from Squamish Canoe Rental.

Squamish Boat Tours FAQ

What are the best boat tours in Squamish?

Our Wildlife Tour (2-3 hours) and Half-Day Explorer (4-5 hours) are the two most popular options, both departing directly from Squamish with a local licensed captain. Full-day and fully custom tours are available for larger groups or special itineraries.

How much do Squamish boat tours cost?

The 2-3 hour Wildlife Tour is $375 for up to 3 guests, plus $79 per additional guest. The 4-5 hour Half-Day Explorer is $625 for up to 3 guests, plus $129 per additional guest. Full-day and custom tours are quoted per trip - call (604) 849-8898.

Where do the tours depart from?

Squamish, BC, at the head of Howe Sound. We also run sunrise and sunset tours departing directly from Porteau Cove, about 20 minutes closer for anyone coming from Vancouver.

Are the tours good for kids and families?

Yes - families are some of our most common bookings. Wildlife watching and calm fjord water make for an easy, engaging trip, and life jackets come in a range of sizes.

Is it a private tour or a shared boat?

Always private. It's your group and the captain - no strangers sharing the boat. That's also why we can flex the pace and route to what you actually want to see.
