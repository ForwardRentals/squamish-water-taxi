The scenic way down the corridor

Lions Bay sits on the busiest, twistiest stretch of the Sea to Sky Highway. By boat you trade traffic for granite walls, waterfalls and open water, and the water stays open when the highway doesn't.

About Lions Bay

Lions Bay is a small village on the east shore of lower Howe Sound, tucked under the peaks it's named after. Its small marina and waterfront make it a useful pick-up point between Squamish and Horseshoe Bay.

Popular Trips

Resident transfers, pick-ups for hikers coming off the Lions trails, highway-closure backup, and scenic runs up to Squamish for visitors staying in the village.

Perfect For

Lions Bay residents, hikers who want a different way home, and visitors who'd rather see the fjord from the water.

Lions Bay Water Taxi FAQ

How long is the boat from Squamish to Lions Bay?

About 17 nautical miles, roughly an hour at cruising speed.

Can you pick us up after a hike?

Yes. Give us your expected finish time and we'll set a pick-up window. Call us if you're running late.

Do you go further south?

Yes. Horseshoe Bay and downtown Vancouver are both on our route map. See the trip planner.
