No ferry runs to Anvil Island

Anvil has no road connection and no scheduled ferry service. A private water taxi is the simplest way out for hikers, cabin owners and anyone who wants a day on one of Howe Sound's most recognizable islands.

About Anvil Island

Anvil Island sits in the middle of upper Howe Sound, its sharp summit easy to pick out from the Sea to Sky Highway. The island's high point, Leading Peak, is a steep climb with views straight down the fjord. It's a hike most people only ever look at from the road.

Popular Trips

Most trips are hiker drop-offs for Leading Peak. Plan a full day and pick a pick-up time with plenty of buffer. Cabin owners use us for supply runs and guest transfers, and some groups simply want a picnic on a quiet beach.

Perfect For

Peak-baggers, photographers, cabin owners moving people and gear, and anyone chasing a Howe Sound island most visitors never set foot on.

Planning Your Trip

Tell us your group size, how much gear you're bringing and when you want to be picked up. We confirm the landing spot for the day's tide and weather. Bring water. There are no services on the island.

What will it cost? Fares depend on your start point, group size and whether it's one way or a round trip. Open the trip planner with Anvil Island already selected for an instant estimate, or call (604) 849-8898 for a firm quote.

Anvil Island Water Taxi FAQ

How do you get to Anvil Island?

By private boat. There's no road and no scheduled ferry. Squamish Water Taxi runs drop-offs and pick-ups from Squamish (about 16.5 nautical miles, roughly 55 minutes) or from other Howe Sound launches like Porteau Cove.

Can you drop us off for the Leading Peak hike?

Yes. That's the most popular Anvil Island trip. We drop you at the shore in the morning and come back at the time you choose. Leave generous time for the climb and the descent.

How many people fit on the water taxi?

Up to 6 passengers per trip, with room for day packs. Let us know about bulky gear or dogs when you book.
