Ekins Point is on the roadless west side of Howe Sound, north of Gambier Island. A private water taxi gets you and your guests there when it suits you. There's no waiting on a ferry and no extra stops.

About Ekins Point

Ekins Point looks out over the upper Sound toward Anvil Island. It's a quiet stretch of waterfront where cabins are reached by water, and the trip in is part of the appeal: fjord walls, seals on the rocks and eagles overhead.

Popular Trips

Weekend cabin arrivals, guest pick-ups, grocery and supply runs, and end-of-season closing trips. We can meet you in Squamish, Porteau Cove or other Howe Sound launches.

Perfect For

Cabin owners, families heading out for the weekend, and guests who'd rather not work out boat logistics themselves.

Planning Your Trip

Tell us the number of people, how much luggage or supplies you have, and your preferred departure time. Friday-evening and Sunday runs book up first in summer.

Ekins Point Water Taxi FAQ

How do you get to Ekins Point?

By boat. It's on the west side of Howe Sound with no road access. From Squamish it's about 17 nautical miles, roughly an hour. From Porteau Cove it's quicker.

Can you pick up guests for us?

Yes. Give us their names and arrival time, and we'll meet them at the launch and bring them out.

What about weather?

Howe Sound can get windy, especially in the afternoon. We watch the forecast and will suggest a better time if conditions are poor. Safety comes first.
