Howe Sound by Boat in Fall and Winter: What to Know Before You Go

October 19, 2026 | Squamish Water Taxi

Most visitors only think about Howe Sound in summer. But the fjord doesn’t close for the season, and off-season trips are available by request. Fall and winter on the water bring snow-capped peaks, quiet anchorages and some of the best light of the year. They also bring colder air, shorter days and stronger winds. Here’s what to expect if you’re thinking about a boat tour or water taxi from Squamish between October and March.

Why the Off-Season Is Worth It

  • Snow on the mountains. The peaks around the Sound get their first snow in fall, and the contrast with the dark water is spectacular.
  • Sunrise without the alarm. From October through mid-February the sun rises after 7 a.m., so a sunrise tour doesn’t mean a 4:30 wake-up.
  • Quiet water. Far fewer boats are out, and the launches and beaches are close to empty.
  • Wildlife. Bald eagles gather around Squamish in late fall and winter, and harbour seals are year-round residents of the Sound. See our Howe Sound wildlife guide.

Weather and Wind

Coastal BC weather changes fast, and Howe Sound has its own patterns. In winter, cold outflow winds can funnel down the Squamish valley and out onto the Sound, making the water choppy and the wind chill bitter. Other days are glassy calm from dawn to dusk.

Your captain checks the Environment Canada marine forecast for Howe Sound before every departure. If conditions aren’t safe, we reschedule. That’s why off-season trips work best with a little flexibility on dates.

Plan Around the Daylight

Days are short in December, with sunset before 4:30 p.m. Morning and midday trips give you the most light. A half-day tour that starts around sunrise is a great way to see the Sound in the soft winter light and be back before dark.

What to Wear

It is always colder on the water than on land, especially when the boat is moving. Dress like you’re heading up the mountain:

  • A warm base layer and an insulated mid layer (wool or synthetic, not cotton)
  • A waterproof, windproof outer jacket
  • A toque, gloves or mitts, and a neck warmer
  • Warm, grippy footwear
  • Sunglasses: low winter sun off the water is bright

We provide a properly fitted life jacket for every passenger. More tips in our guide to what to wear and bring on a Howe Sound boat trip.

Good Off-Season Trips

  • Sunrise and sunset tours, including departures from Porteau Cove.

  • Photography charters built around the best winter light. Ask us for a quote.
