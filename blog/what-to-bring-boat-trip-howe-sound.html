What to Wear and Bring on a Howe Sound Boat Trip

October 19, 2026 | Squamish Water Taxi

Booked a Howe Sound boat tour or water taxi and wondering what to pack? Our boat is an open RIB, which means fresh air, big views and nothing between you and the fjord, including the wind. A little preparation makes the difference between a great trip and a chilly one. Here’s our list.

1. Layers, Even in Summer

A warm July day in Squamish can feel 10 degrees cooler on the water once the boat is moving, and the afternoon wind on the Sound is often strong in summer. Bring:

  • A light warm layer like a fleece or puffy
  • A windproof, ideally waterproof, jacket
  • A hat that won’t blow away, or a toque in the cooler months

2. Sun Protection

Sun reflects off the water, so you’ll burn faster than you expect. Sunscreen, sunglasses (polarized ones cut the glare and help you spot seals), and a hat with a strap or chin cord.

3. A Dry Bag for Your Phone and Camera

Spray happens. A small dry bag or a zip-lock bag keeps your phone, wallet and keys safe. For photos, a camera strap or wrist strap is a good idea; things dropped over the side of a boat are gone for good. A zoom lens helps for wildlife, since we keep a respectful distance from seals and birds.

4. Grippy Shoes

Wet decks are slippery. Wear closed-toe shoes or sandals with a good grip, especially if you’re being dropped at a beach or trailhead like Echo Falls, where you may step out onto rocks or sand.

5. If You Get Seasick

Howe Sound is a sheltered fjord and usually calmer than the open ocean, but wind can stir it up. If you’re prone to motion sickness, take your preferred remedy before the trip, eat something light beforehand, and keep your eyes on the horizon.

6. Water and Snacks

Bring a water bottle and a snack, especially for half-day and full-day tours, or if we’re dropping you off for a hike.

What We Provide

  • A properly fitted life jacket for every passenger
  • All Transport Canada required safety equipment, checked before every departure
  • A licensed local captain who knows the Sound

Quick Checklist

  • Warm layer and windproof jacket
  • Hat, sunglasses, sunscreen
  • Dry bag for phone and valuables
  • Grippy closed-toe shoes
  • Water and a snack
  • Seasickness remedy if you need one

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide life jackets?

Yes. Every passenger gets a properly fitted life jacket, and the boat carries all Transport Canada required safety gear, checked before every departure.

Will I get wet on the boat?

It's an open boat, so a bit of spray is possible, especially on windy days. A waterproof jacket and a dry bag for electronics keep you comfortable.

Can I bring my dog?

Dogs are welcome on our Echo Falls shuttle. For tours and other trips, mention your dog when you book and we'll let you know.
