Proposals, Birthdays & Anniversaries: Private Boat Tours on Howe Sound

October 21, 2026 | Squamish Water Taxi

Some days deserve more than dinner and a card. A private boat tour on Howe Sound gives you granite walls, waterfalls, wildlife and golden light, with nobody else on board but your people and a local captain.

Proposals

A quiet cove, the mountains glowing at sunset, and nobody watching. It's hard to beat. A few tips:

  • Tell us in advance. We'll plan the route and timing so you're somewhere beautiful at the right moment.
  • Book sunset (or sunrise). Calm water, soft light, and great photos.
  • Keep the ring safe. A zipped inside pocket, not a jacket pocket you'll be reaching into on a moving boat.
  • Have a weather backup. We'll only go if conditions are safe, so have a second date in mind.

Birthdays

A boat trip is a great birthday for the person who doesn't want a party. Make it a Wildlife Tour with a picnic, or a Half-Day Explorer with stops at beaches and coves.

Anniversaries and honeymoons

Visiting the Sea to Sky for an anniversary or honeymoon? A sunset tour from Porteau Cove is an easy add-on to a Whistler or Squamish stay, and it's 20 minutes closer to Vancouver than Squamish.

Family celebrations and visitors

Showing family from out of town what the Sea to Sky is all about? A boat trip shows them the side of Howe Sound you can't see from the highway. It's also a good option for anyone who can't hike to the viewpoints.

Photo shoots

Engagement photos, family portraits and content shoots on the water look incredible, especially at golden hour. Let us know you're bringing a photographer so we can plan stops for the best light and angles.

Choosing a tour

  • Howe Sound Wildlife Tour: 2-3 hours, $375 for up to 3 guests ($79 per extra guest). Perfect for a sunset proposal.
  • Half-Day Howe Sound Explorer: 4-5 hours, $625 for up to 3 guests ($129 per extra guest). Time for a picnic and beach stops.
  • Full-day and custom charters: built around your plans, quoted per trip.

Sunrise and sunset departures are available on every tour, from Squamish or Porteau Cove.

Planning tips

  • Book early for summer weekends.
  • Dress in warm layers. It's always colder on the water. See what to wear and bring.
  • Bring a dry bag for phones and cameras.
  • Life jackets and safety gear are provided.
  • Be flexible: your captain checks the marine forecast before every trip and will reschedule if it's not safe.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I propose on a boat tour in Squamish?

Yes. Private tours are just your group and the captain, so a proposal at a quiet spot on Howe Sound is easy to arrange. Tell us ahead of time and we'll help with timing and the location.

What is the best time for a special occasion boat tour?

Sunrise and sunset departures are the most memorable, with calm water and golden light. They're available on request from Squamish or Porteau Cove.

How many people can come on a private boat tour?

Tours are priced for up to 3 guests, with extra guests at $79 per person on the Wildlife Tour and $129 on longer tours. Call us with your group size.
