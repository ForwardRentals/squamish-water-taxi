Same Boat, Different Lake.

The boat that runs our Howe Sound tours and rentals trailers up to Whistler on request for wakeboarding and wake surfing on Alta Lake - glassy morning water and mountains all around.

Wakeboard or Wake Surf?

Wakeboarding is a board with foot bindings, towed behind the boat on a rope - you carve back and forth across the wake at speed. Wake surfing uses a shorter, finless-feeling board and no rope once you're up - the boat idles along and you ride the standing wave right off the back. Both are on the table for your session.

Wakeboarding FAQ

Is there wakeboarding in Whistler?

Yes. We trailer our boat up to Alta Lake, the main lake in the Whistler townsite, for captain-driven wakeboarding and wake surfing. Green Lake is also an option depending on conditions and access.

What's the difference between wakeboarding and wake surfing?

Wakeboarding uses a board with foot bindings and a tow rope, carving back and forth across the wake at speed. Wake surfing uses a shorter, finless-feeling board and no rope once you're up - you ride directly in the pocket of the wake behind a slower-moving boat.

Do I need experience?

No - our captain will get you set up and coach you through getting up on the board. Sessions work for first-timers and experienced riders alike.

How do I book a session?

This one's request-based rather than instant online booking - call (604) 849-8898 or email squamishwatertaxi@gmail.com with your group size and preferred dates and we'll confirm availability.

When's the best time to go?

Summer, and early morning water is usually glassiest before the wind picks up - that makes for a smoother wake and an easier time riding.
