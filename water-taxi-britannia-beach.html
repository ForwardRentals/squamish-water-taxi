When the highway isn't an option

When Highway 99 closes or backs up, the water is still open. A water taxi between Squamish and Britannia Beach keeps people moving, and on a calm day it's a far better view than the dashboard.

About Britannia Beach

Britannia Beach is the highway village just south of Squamish, home to the Britannia Mine Museum and a stretch of Howe Sound shoreline under steep granite walls. It's the closest stop on the Sound to Squamish.

Popular Trips

Short transfers and pick-ups, backup transport during highway closures, and the first leg of longer Howe Sound trips. It's also a quick scenic run for visitors who want a taste of the Sound.

Perfect For

Locals dealing with a highway closure, visitors combining the mine museum with time on the water, and anyone who wants a short, scenic hop.

Britannia Beach Water Taxi FAQ

How long is the boat from Squamish to Britannia Beach?

About 6.6 nautical miles, roughly 20 minutes at cruising speed, depending on conditions.

Do you run during Highway 99 closures?

When conditions allow, yes. Call us as early as you can. See our guide to marine transport during Highway 99 closures.

Can we keep going past Britannia?

Of course. Britannia is the first stop on the way to Porteau Cove, the islands and beyond. Plan any route on the trip planner.
